Delete Your Account

Teacher Parent App · Tecvo (Pty) Ltd
Last updated: 30 May 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Teacher Parent App account and the personal information associated with it. The App is published by Tecvo (Pty) Ltd.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Teacher Parent App and sign in.
  2. Tap the Settings tab at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when prompted. You are signed out immediately, your sign-in is disabled, and your data is removed from the app and hidden from all other users.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you cannot access the App, email support@teacherparentapp.co.za from the email address on your account, with the subject line “Delete my account”. We will verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is removed from the app

When your account is deleted, the following are removed from the app and hidden from all other users, and your photos are deleted from file storage:

  • Your profile details (title, name, email address, phone number, gender, biography, qualifications and education history)
  • Your profile photograph and any child profile photographs you uploaded
  • Children's details you added (names, grade/class, attendance records and emergency contact number)
  • Direct messages, comments, and any homework, tests, assignments, announcements or other content you posted
  • Files and documents you uploaded
  • Your sign-in (authentication) record — you can no longer log in

What is kept, and for how long

The Teacher Parent App also serves as a record of school communications, so that a parent or teacher can later show what was (or was not) shared. For that reason, when you delete your account a single copy of your school-communication records (posts, attendance and messages) is kept in a restricted internal archive and is then permanently and automatically deleted after 12 months.

This archive is not visible to any app user — not to teachers, not to parents, not even to you. It is accessible only to Tecvo (Pty) Ltd, under access controls, and is used only to resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Anonymised, aggregated crash and performance diagnostics that cannot identify you may also be retained to keep the App stable. If you want your records permanently erased before the 12-month period ends, email us and we will erase them unless we are legally required to keep them.

Once deleted you can no longer sign in, and your information disappears from other users' devices. To use Teacher Parent App again afterwards, you would need to register a new account.
Questions? Contact support@teacherparentapp.co.za.
