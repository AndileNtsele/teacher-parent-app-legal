Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 May 2026
Last updated: 19 May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tecvo (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use the Teacher Parent App ("the App"). The App is designed to connect parents of South African school learners with their child's classroom by giving teachers a structured channel to share homework, tests, assignments, exams, sports updates, meetings, announcements and attendance information directly with parents.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) of the Republic of South Africa, as well as the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide on registration

When you register as a teacher, we collect:

  • Title, first name, surname
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Password (stored only as an encrypted hash by Firebase Authentication)
  • School information (name, EMIS number, province) — selected from public Department of Basic Education data
  • Grades, sub-grades (classes) and subjects you teach
  • Optional: profile photograph, biography, professional qualifications, education history, prior teaching schools

When you register as a parent, we collect:

  • Title, first name, surname
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Password (stored only as an encrypted hash by Firebase Authentication)
  • Optional: profile photograph

2.2 Information about your child (provided by parents)

When a parent registers a child, the parent provides the child's first name, surname, grade, sub-grade (class) and school. Parents may add multiple children. Children do not register or use the App themselves. All child information is entered, controlled and may be deleted by the parent at any time from within the App.

2.3 Information generated by your use of the App

  • Content posted by teachers: homework, tests, assignments, exams, sports updates, meetings, announcements, attached PDF documents
  • Attendance records: daily present/absent markings for learners in the teacher's class
  • Comments made by parents and teachers on posted content
  • Direct messages exchanged between a parent and a teacher about a specific child and subject
  • Read receipts and timestamps for the above

2.4 Technical information

We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (provided by Google LLC) which automatically processes a unique user identifier (UID), device authentication tokens, and IP address for the purpose of authenticating sessions and securing your account. We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs that profile users, or third-party trackers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Create and manage your account (parent and/or teacher)
  • Match a parent to the teachers of their child's exact class and subjects, and deliver classroom content (homework, tests, etc.) to the correct parents
  • Enable direct messaging between a teacher and a parent regarding a specific child
  • Display attendance records to a parent for their own child
  • Send transactional notifications (e.g. a new homework post, a reply to a message)
  • Maintain the security of your account and detect abuse
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal effects.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (POPIA)

We process personal information on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — given when you complete registration and tick the Terms & Privacy acknowledgement
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the App's functionality to you
  • Legitimate interest — to keep the service secure and to improve it
  • Legal obligation — where required by South African law

5. Children's Personal Information

The App is intended for use by parents and teachers (adults aged 18 and over). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from any child. Information about a child is entered by their parent on the parent's own device, under the parent's account, and remains under the parent's control.

A parent may at any time:

  • View what information is held about each child
  • Edit or correct a child's information
  • Remove a child from their account
  • Delete their entire account (which deletes all of that parent's children from our systems)

Teachers receive content posted to their class and may see the names of children in their own class register for attendance purposes. Teachers do not see other children's information.

6. Who Has Access to Your Information

6.1 Other users of the App

  • A teacher can see the names of learners in the exact class they teach, the names of those learners' parents who have registered, and content/messages those parents send to that teacher.
  • A parent can see the names, profile photo, biography and professional details of teachers who teach their child, and content/messages those teachers send.
  • The App uses a strict "box" matching system: a teacher and a parent can communicate only if they share the same exact combination of school, grade, class and subject.

6.2 Service providers

We use the following sub-processors to operate the App. They process personal information strictly on our instructions:

  • Google LLC — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging (Firebase Privacy)

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party, and we do not share it for marketing purposes.

7. Cross-border Transfers

Firebase services may store and process data on servers located outside the Republic of South Africa, including in the European Union and the United States. Google LLC is bound by appropriate contractual safeguards. By using the App you consent to such cross-border processing as permitted under section 72 of POPIA.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • Account information is kept for as long as your account is active.
  • Content (homework, tests, messages, attendance) is kept for as long as the author's account is active, or until the author deletes it.
  • When you delete your account from within the App, your profile and all content authored by you is removed from our active databases within 30 days. Backups containing the data are overwritten on a rolling 90-day cycle.

9. Security

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and at rest (Firebase managed encryption)
  • Authentication via Firebase Authentication with hashed passwords
  • Server-side access rules that prevent users from reading data outside their own class/child scope
  • Restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

10. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Be informed that we hold your personal information and what we do with it
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Delete your account and personal information (available in-app under Settings → Delete Account)
  • Object to processing of your information in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent previously given (this may mean you can no longer use the App)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator: inforegulator.org.za · complaints.IR@justice.gov.za

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@teacherparentapp.co.za.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Material changes will be notified to you within the App.
